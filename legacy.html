流行文化衍生

Legacy in Popular Culture —— 从魔典到 ACG：七十二柱如何成为共享的奇幻符号库。

走出魔典

二十世纪以来，所罗门七十二柱逐渐走出秘传魔典，进入大众文化。它们不再是仪式手册里的名讳，而成了创作者随手取用的「恶魔图鉴」：每一位都有名字、爵位、封印与一项鲜明的权能，天然适合被塑造成角色、敌人或召唤兽。

Since the twentieth century the seventy-two have left the esoteric grimoire for popular culture. No longer mere ritual names, they became a ready-made "demon encyclopaedia": each with a name, a rank, a seal and one vivid power — naturally fitted to be turned into characters, antagonists or summoned creatures.

游戏与动漫

在日本 ACG 中，「七十二柱魔神」几乎成了一个固定标签。从《女神转生》系列里可作为仲魔收服的恶魔，到《Fate》系列中人格化的所罗门体系，再到各类作品把派蒙（Paimon）、巴巴托斯（Barbatos）、阿斯摩德（Asmodeus）等名字赋予具体角色——七十二柱成为了一套可无限重组的世界观素材。

In Japanese anime and games, the "seventy-two pillars" have become something of a fixed tag. From the demons recruitable as allies in the Megami Tensei series to the personified Solomonic system of Fate, to the countless works that give concrete characters the names Paimon, Barbatos, Asmodeus and the rest — the seventy-two offer an endlessly re-combinable lore kit.

影视与文学

西方影视与文学同样反复借用这一谱系：阿斯摩德、彼列（Belial）、亚蒙（Amon）等名字频繁现身于奇幻、恐怖与都市传说题材。它们或被写成堕天使，或被塑造成精于算计的反派，甚至成为神秘学题材中「契约与代价」叙事的道具。

Western film and literature borrow the same lineage: Asmodeus, Belial, Amon and others recur throughout fantasy, horror and urban legend. They are written as fallen angels, as scheming antagonists, or as the props of "pact and price" narratives in occult thrillers.

一个共享的符号库

值得注意的是，流行文化中的七十二柱往往与原始魔典相去甚远——封印、位阶甚至性格都被自由改写。它们承载的，与其说是某种「真实」的恶魔学，不如说是人类对「召唤、契约与代价」这一古老母题的持续想象。

It is worth noting that the seventy-two of popular culture often stray far from the original grimoires — their seals, ranks and even temperaments freely rewritten. What they carry is less a "real" demonology than humanity's abiding imagination of the old motif of summons, pacts and their price.
