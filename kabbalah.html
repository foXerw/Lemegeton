Kabbalah & the Angels

卡巴拉与七十二天使

Shem HaMephorash —— 神的七十二字名，与所罗门七十二魔互为镜像的传统。

七十二字名

在犹太卡巴拉传统中，《出埃及记》14 章 19–21 节三节经文各含 72 个希伯来字母。将三节字母按特定方式交错排列，可展开出 72 个三字母组合；再各加上神名的后缀（-el 或 -iah），便得到 72 位天使之名——这就是「Shem HaMephorash」（显化的圣名）。这 72 位天使平分黄道 360 度，每位执掌 5 度，各有其属性与管辖。

In the Kabbalah, Exodus 14:19–21 each contain seventy-two Hebrew letters. Interlacing the three verses in a set manner unfolds seventy-two three-letter combinations; adding a divine suffix (-el or -iah) to each yields the names of seventy-two angels — the "Shem HaMephorash," the explicit Name. These angels divide the 360 degrees of the zodiac, each ruling five degrees with its own office and province.

黑暗的镜像

所罗门七十二魔的数目，常被后世神秘学者视作这七十二天使的「黑暗镜像」——一面是神圣之名展开出的光明秩序，一面是召魔手册中的黑暗名录。这一对称极具想象力，但也必须说明：把每一位恶魔与同序号的天使一一对应，是现代的阐释性做法，并非古代正典。下表即按此「序号对应」列出，供参照。

Later occultists often read the seventy-two demons of Solomon as a "dark mirror" of these seventy-two angels — a luminous order unfolded from the divine Name on one side, a catalogue of darkness on the other. The symmetry is striking, but it must be said plainly: pairing each demon with the angel of the same number is a modern interpretive device, not ancient canon. 黄金黎明与克劳利

十九世纪末的黄金黎明会（Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn）把卡巴拉、塔罗与仪式魔法整合成一套严密的符号体系，所罗门的七十二魔与七十二天使都在其中获得新位置。阿莱斯特·克劳利（Aleister Crowley）随后编注了 Goetia，重绘封印、补注灵体信息，使这一谱系成为现代神秘学与流行文化共用的底本。

At the close of the nineteenth century the Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn wove Kabbalah, tarot and ceremonial magic into one rigorous symbolic system, giving both the seventy-two demons and the seventy-two angels a new place. Aleister Crowley then edited the Goetia, redrawing seals and annotating the spirits, fixing the lineage as common stock for both modern occultism and popular culture.
