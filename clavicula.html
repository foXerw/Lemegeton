The Greater Key

CLAVIS · MAGNA

大钥匙 · 所罗门之钥

Clavicula Salomonis —— 一部聚焦天使、行星精灵与护符制作的文艺复兴仪式魔法手册。

成书与流传

《所罗门之钥》（Clavicula Salomonis）成书于十四至十五世纪，现存手稿多为意大利文或希腊文，托名所罗门，实为文艺复兴时期仪式魔法的汇编。它与《小钥匙》相比基调更「白」：重心不在召魔，而在召唤天使与行星精灵、净化自身、以及制作护符。十六世纪后它被译为拉丁文、法文、英文，广泛流传，成为西方仪式魔法的经典范本。

The Key of Solomon (Clavicula Salomonis) was compiled in the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries; its surviving manuscripts are largely Italian or Greek. Written under Solomon's name, it is in truth a Renaissance anthology of ceremonial magic. Lighter in tone than the Lesser Key, it centres on summoning angels and planetary spirits, self-purification and the making of talismans. After the sixteenth century it spread widely in Latin, French and English translations.

行星魔法

全书以七曜——日、月、金、木、水、火、土——为骨架。每一颗行星都对应特定的时辰、熏香、金属、颜色与符文；施术者须在对应行星的「行星时」行事。这套体系源自新柏拉图主义与占星术：天上星辰的运行被认为与地上万物相应，而魔法正是借由「对应」来调动这些力量。

The work is organised around the seven planets — Sun, Moon, Venus, Jupiter, Mercury, Mars and Saturn. Each planet carries its own hour, incense, metal, colour and characters, and the operator must act within the planet's appointed "planetary hour." The system descends from Neoplatonism and astrology: the motions of the heavens answer to earthly things, and magic works by drawing on those correspondences.

五芒星护符

《所罗门之钥》最著名的是它的「五芒星」（Pentacles）——刻于金属或羊皮纸上的圆盘形护符，每一枚对应一颗行星并服务于特定目的：招财、护身、驱邪、求得爱情或智慧。书中详列护符的图形、铭文与制作时的洁净条件。后世流行的「所罗门护符」图像，大多即出于此。

Best known are its "pentacles" — disc-shaped talismans engraved on metal or parchment, each bound to a planet and serving a specific purpose: wealth, protection, exorcism, love or wisdom. The book sets out their figures, inscriptions and the conditions of purity required to make them. Most later images of "Solomonic talismans" derive from here.

净化与祝圣

与大钥匙相伴的是一整套预备仪式：沐浴斋戒、洁净场地、祝圣工具（刀、杖、杯、剑）、划定魔法圆。全书反复强调施术者自身的「纯洁」——这一伦理框架使《所罗门之钥》更接近一套虔诚的宗教实践，而非单纯的巫术。

With the Key goes a whole apparatus of preparation: bathing and fasting, cleansing the place, consecrating the tools — knife, wand, cup, staff — and drawing the magic circle. Throughout, the text insists on the operator's own "purity." That moral frame brings the Key closer to a pious religious practice than to mere sorcery.

书的结构：一部操作手册的目录

通行的《所罗门之钥》（Mathers 据大英博物馆 Sloane 抄本译出的两卷本）结构极工整，几乎是一部「开工前 checklist」：第一卷讲人——施术者的斋戒与洁净、每日祈祷文、复明与驱魔的祷词；第二卷讲物与场——祭坛的祝圣、魔法圆的画法与铭文、各类兵刃（刀、剑、杖）的选材与锻造、圣油与墨水的配方、香炉与蜡烛，最后才是五芒星护符（Pentacles）与各行星的符纸。整部书的重心耐人寻味：真正「做法」的章节很少，绝大多数篇幅在准备——祝圣一项工具就要占去数页。这正是仪式魔法的性格：工作九成发生在开始之前。

The standard Key (the two books Mathers translated from Sloane manuscripts in the British Museum) is built like a pre-operation checklist. Book I concerns the operator: fasts and washings, daily prayers, orisons for illumination and exorcism. Book II concerns things and places: the consecration of the altar, the drawing and lettering of the circle, the choice and forging of the blades — knife, sword, wand — recipes for holy oil and ink, censer and candle — and only then the pentacles and the planetary characts. Where the book spends its bulk is telling: actual "operations" are few; most pages are preparation, and the consecration of a single tool can take several chapters. This is the temperament of ceremonial magic — ninety percent of the work happens before it begins.

洁净礼：把身体变成法场

《大钥匙》的预备程序细到苛刻：选定吉日后，施术者须自初一起斋戒九日（或至少三日），每日晨昏诵指定的祷文与诗篇；行法前夜沐浴，着专门的白麻法衣与法冠——衣冠本身也要祝圣；场地要洒水洁净（用祝圣过的圣水与圣油），祭坛朝东，蜡烛与香炉各就其位。书中甚至规定了随身之物：不带铁器入圆、不佩妇女的饰物、鞋须新制。这套繁琐礼节的意义，是把「日常之人」暂时改造成「合法身份」——正如法官须穿法袍、法庭须挂国徽，洁净礼是给施术者发放的「证件」。

The preparation is punishingly fine: after choosing an auspicious day the operator fasts nine days (at minimum three) from the new moon, reciting appointed prayers and psalms morning and evening; he bathes the night before the working and dons a dedicated white linen robe and mitre — themselves consecrated; the ground is sprinkled with holy water and oil, the altar faces east, candle and censer take their stations. The book even dictates what may be carried: no iron within the circle, no women's ornaments, shoes newly made. The point of all this protocol is to convert the everyday man into a legal status — as the judge must robe and the courtroom must bear its emblem, the purification is the operator's warrant.

行星对应表：七曜的行政辖区

大钥匙的宇宙由七颗行星分管，每颗行星有一整套「辖区特产」：日、金属、颜色、香料、时辰。操作什么事务，就取什么行星的时段、金属与颜色——求财用木星，求爱用金星，束缚与隐匿用土星。这套对应不是装饰，而是整套技术的传动轴：护符用对应金属铸造、在对应时辰祝圣、以对应颜色包裹，力量才算「接对了线」。

The Key's cosmos is administered by the seven planets, each with a whole catalogue of "district produce": day, metal, colour, incense, hour. Whatever the errand, one takes that planet's watch, metal and colour — Jupiter for wealth, Venus for love, Saturn for binding and concealment. The correspondence is ornament only on the surface; underneath it is the drive-shaft of the whole technique: a talisman cast in its planet's metal, consecrated in its planet's hour, wrapped in its planet's colour — only then is the circuit correctly wired.

九组五芒星护符

《大钥匙》最出名的遗产是它的「五芒星」（Pentacles）——一套按行星分组的符图，通常刊本列为九组：第一组属土星（束缚、驱逐、致郁），其后依次为木星（财富与尊荣）、火星（战争与护身）、太阳（健康与光荣）、金星（爱情与友谊）、水星（学识与雄辩）等，每组含数枚符图，各注明用途、材质、时辰与祝圣词。符图上环绕的多是神名与「野蛮之名」，中央常为几何图形或圣号。它们与 Goetia 的恶魔封印形制相似，功能却相反：恶魔封印是「点名拘传」，行星护符是「接电取能」。后世坊间流传的「所罗门护符」，十有八九即出自这套谱系。

The Key's most famous legacy is its Pentacles — a set of figures grouped by planet, conventionally printed in nine series: the first of Saturn (binding, driving away, melancholy), then Jupiter (wealth and honor), Mars (war and protection), the Sun (health and glory), Venus (love and friendship), Mercury (learning and eloquence), each series holding several characts with their purpose, material, hour and consecration. Their borders run with divine and barbarous Names; their centers are geometric or sacred signs. They resemble the Goetic seals in form and invert them in function: a demon's seal is a subpoena; a planetary pentacle is a power line. Most "Solomonic talismans" current today descend from this very set.

Suffrages：代祷的系统

大钥匙里另有一类常被忽略的操作：suffrages（代祷）。它不召唤、不约束，而是以固定的短祷文请求七大天使长（米迦勒、加百列、拉斐尔等）与四位「大亲王」的代祷与协助——求智慧、求辩才、求寻物、求见神宠。文本格式近乎礼拜经课：每段以「Oremus（让我们祈祷）」起，以阿门结。也就是说，《大钥匙》的灵界接口其实有两种：对天使，用祈祷；对较低级的灵，才用命令。这个分层正是「白魔法」的定义——它自始至终没有越出「求告」的语法。

A neglected class of operations in the Key are the suffrages: no summoning, no binding, but fixed short prayers asking the seven archangels (Michael, Gabriel, Raphael and the rest) and the four great princes for intercession and aid — for wisdom, for eloquence, for finding things, for favor. The rubrics read like a liturgy: each opens "Oremus" and closes with Amen. The Key, in other words, has two interfaces to spirit-world: prayer for angels, command only for the lower orders. That layering is the very definition of its "white magic" — it never leaves the grammar of petition.

与 Goetia 对照：同一工具间的两条流水线

把两部「钥匙」并置，能看清所罗门体系的全貌：同一套神名、同一种洁净礼、同一个圆，却接了两条方向相反的流水线。《大钥匙》向上：祈祷、祝圣、佩戴护符，求的是天使的代祷与行星的德性，几乎不涉恶魔；《小钥匙》向下：点名、约束、谈判、遣散，直接差遣被囚的恶魔办事。器具也相应分工——大钥匙用白麻衣、圣水、乳香，时辰取行星吉时；Goetia 用铜碟焚香、没药与安息香，画圆念三段召词。两条路共享的，是那个更根本的信念：人，在满足条件之后，可以合法地调用灵界。

Set side by side, the two Keys show the whole Solomonic system: the same Names, the same purification, the same circle — feeding two pipelines running in opposite directions. The Greater Key runs upward: prayer, consecration, talismans, asking the suffrages of angels and the virtues of planets, barely touching demons. The Lesser Key runs downward: naming, binding, bargaining, dismissing — direct commissions to the bound spirits. The kit divides accordingly — linen, holy water and frankincense for the Key, chosen in planetary hours; the brazen censer, myrrh and storax, the circle and the three adjurations for the Goetia. What both share is the deeper conviction: that man, being qualified, may lawfully draw on the spirit-world.
