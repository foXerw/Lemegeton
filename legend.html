The Legend

ANVLVS · SALOMONIS

所罗门的传说

The Legend of Solomon —— 一枚指环、一座圣殿、一口铜瓶，以及一个名字如何成为「魔法师之王」的符号。

指环与恶魔

传说以色列王所罗门（Solomon）拥有一枚刻有神名与六芒星的指环——「所罗门之印」（Seal of Solomon）。凭这枚指环，他号令群魔、役使精灵，令它们俯首听命。犹太、伊斯兰与基督教传统都流传着所罗门驾御恶魔的故事：在犹太传说中，指环上镌刻着不可言说的四字神名；在伊斯兰传统中，指环上则是万有之主的印记。

By legend, King Solomon bore a ring engraved with the Name and a six-pointed star — the Seal of Solomon — by which he commanded demons and spirits to his will. Jewish, Islamic and Christian traditions each tell of Solomon's mastery over demons: in Jewish lore the ring bore the ineffable four-letter Name; in Islamic tradition it carried the sign of the Lord of all.

圣殿

最著名的故事，是所罗门驱使恶魔搬运巨石、开凿山岩，以修建耶路撒冷圣殿。传说恶魔之首亚什莫代（Asmodeus，即图鉴中的第 32 柱）也曾被所罗门俘获，为其服役。这些故事把「神圣的建筑工程」与「被征服的黑暗力量」编织在一起，成为后世魔典的核心意象。

The best-known tale has Solomon pressing demons into hauling stone and hewing rock to raise the Temple in Jerusalem. Even Asmodeus — the 32nd spirit of the catalogue — was said to have been captured and made to serve. These stories weave together sacred construction and conquered darkness, the core image of the later grimoires.

铜瓶

圣殿落成后，传说所罗门将不顺服的恶魔尽数封入一口铜瓶，以神名封印，沉入巴比伦的深渊。这一意象后来在《一千零一夜》中化为「渔夫与魔鬼」的著名故事——一个被封印千年的精灵从瓶中释放。它承载着一层核心观念：恶魔可以被「召唤、约束与封印」，这正是后世魔法手册的前提。

When the Temple was done, Solomon is said to have sealed the disobedient spirits in a brazen vessel, closed with the Name, and cast it into the deeps of Babylon. The image passed into the Arabian Nights as "The Fisherman and the Jinni" — a spirit freed after a thousand years. It carries the central premise of the later handbooks: that demons may be summoned, bound and sealed.

名字为何归于所罗门

严格说，《所罗门之钥》与《所罗门的小钥匙》都不是所罗门本人所写——它们成书于十四至十七世纪，属于「伪经」（pseudepigrapha），即假托古代圣贤之名以增权威。中世纪与文艺复兴的抄写者借用所罗门之名，把散落的召魔技艺编纂成体系，使所罗门成为「魔法师之王」的文化符号。

Strictly, neither Key was written by Solomon — they were compiled between the fourteenth and seventeenth centuries as pseudepigrapha, works borrowing an ancient sage's name for authority. Medieval and Renaissance scribes gathered scattered conjuring arts under Solomon's name, fixing him as the cultural figure of the magician-king.

《所罗门遗训》：最早的剧本

这套传说其实有一部更早的「剧本」——《所罗门遗训》（Testamentum Salomonis），成书约在一至三世纪的希腊语犹太-基督教圈，比所有魔典都早了一千多年。书中，恶魔们被带来为圣殿采石服役，一个名叫 Ornias 的恶魔抗拒役使、骚扰一名少年，所罗门遂以神之名将其拘捕。Ornias 供出了自己的同伙：一个个恶魔报上名来，说明自己「被什么名字驱赶」「害怕什么」——风魔 Asmodeus 与多神教的渊源、夜魔、病魔、引诱人堕落的色魔，逐一自报家门。这份「恶魔自供清单」正是后世七十二柱名讳、职司与「惧怕某名」结构的远祖：Goetia 里每位恶魔「以某圣名受约束」的写法，直接承袭自遗训的问答体。

The legend has an earlier "script" — the Testament of Solomon (Testamentum Salomonis), composed in Greek in Jewish-Christian circles around the first to third centuries, more than a millennium before any grimoire. In it the demons are pressed into service for the Temple's quarrying; a spirit named Ornias resists and harasses a youth, and Solomon seizes him in the Name. Ornias then gives up his associates: one demon after another reports his own name, what drives him and what he fears — Asmodeus and his ties to polytheism, wind-demons, night-demons, lust-demons that snare men into sin. This "roll of confessions" is the distant ancestor of the seventy-two's structure of names, offices and "bound by such-and-such Name": the Goetia's habit of tagging each spirit with the holy name that constrains him descends directly from the Testament's interrogatories.

古兰经中的苏莱曼

所罗门驾御灵体的传说不只属于犹太-基督教传统。在伊斯兰传统中，他是先知兼君王苏莱曼（Sulaymān），蒙真主赐予号令精灵（jinn）、风与鸟兽的权柄：风为他朝发夕至，精灵为他潜水采宝、建造宫室——《古兰经》「精灵章」与「蚂蚁章」都记其事。传说他有一枚镶着宝石的指环，环上刻着真主的大名，凭此统御精灵；他病重时曾倚杖而立整整一年，精灵们不敢察觉他已死，仍继续劳作——这个故事被反复用来讲「幽玄唯真主所知」。犹太、基督、伊斯兰三条传统各自独立地保留了「所罗门约束恶魔」的核心母题，这正是后世魔典得以假托其名的土壤：他的名字在三教里都已经是「人类驾驭灵界」的通用凭证。

Solomon's mastery over spirits is not a Jewish-Christian monopoly. In Islam he is the prophet-king Sulaymān, granted by God authority over the jinn, the wind and the beasts of field and air: the wind carries him morning to evening and back, jinn dive for pearls and raise his palaces — the Qur'an's chapters of the Jinn and the Ant both tell of it. He is given a gem-studded ring bearing the Greatest Name, by which the jinn serve; when he died, it is said, he stood leaning on his staff for a full year, and the jinn, not perceiving his death, kept on working — a story endlessly retold to show that the Unseen is God's alone. Three traditions, independently, all preserved the core motif of Solomon binding demons. That is precisely the soil in which the grimoires could borrow his name: in all three faiths his name already served as the universal warrant for "man commanding the spirit-world."

从驱魔仪式到恶魔名录

中世纪晚期，欧洲出现了一个奇特的文体：恶魔名录（demon lists）。1563 年，约翰·维耶（Johann Weyer）——一位行医的学者，也是少数为「被指控行巫者」辩护的人——在《伪单子恶魔论》（Pseudomonarchia Daemonum）中列出了 69 位恶魔的名讳、封号与召唤法，并附一句耐人寻味的按语：这些不过是无知者的幻觉。这份「既认真记录、又声明存疑」的名录，几乎原样被抄进了后来的《小钥匙》：Goetia 七十二柱的名讳、爵位、显现方式，与维耶的清单高度重合。再往前，1409 年的《巴黎魔典》（Malleus Maleficarum 之前的恶魔学文献）与各种驱魔手册，也都积累了「恶魔各有其名、各有其职」的知识。可以说，七十二柱不是某位天才的发明，而是中世纪驱魔实践、学者目录学与所罗门传说三条线拧成的辫子——到十七世纪，它终于被编成一部「可以查号办事」的完整手册。

In the late Middle Ages Europe produced a peculiar genre: the demon list. In 1563 Johann Weyer — a practicing physician and one of the very few to defend those accused of witchcraft — published the Pseudomonarchia Daemonum, cataloguing sixty-nine demons with their titles, forms and rites of summoning, and appending a pointed note: all this, he said, is the hallucination of the ignorant. That catalogue — recorded in earnest, disavowed in the same breath — passed almost intact into the later Lesser Key: the names, dignities and apparitions of the Goetia's seventy-two overlap heavily with Weyer's roll. Further back, fifteenth-century demonological tracts and the manuals of exorcism had long been accumulating the knowledge that demons each have their name and their office. The seventy-two pillars, in other words, are no one's invention: they are a braid of medieval exorcist practice, scholarly list-making and the Solomonic legend, plaited at last, in the seventeenth century, into a complete handbook one could "dial by number."
