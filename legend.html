The Legend

所罗门的传说

The Legend of Solomon —— 一枚指环、一座圣殿、一口铜瓶，以及一个名字如何成为「魔法师之王」的符号。

指环与恶魔

传说以色列王所罗门（Solomon）拥有一枚刻有神名与六芒星的指环——「所罗门之印」（Seal of Solomon）。凭这枚指环，他号令群魔、役使精灵，令它们俯首听命。犹太、伊斯兰与基督教传统都流传着所罗门驾御恶魔的故事：在犹太传说中，指环上镌刻着不可言说的四字神名；在伊斯兰传统中，指环上则是万有之主的印记。

By legend, King Solomon bore a ring engraved with the Name and a six-pointed star — the Seal of Solomon — by which he commanded demons and spirits to his will. Jewish, Islamic and Christian traditions each tell of Solomon's mastery over demons: in Jewish lore the ring bore the ineffable four-letter Name; in Islamic tradition it carried the sign of the Lord of all.

圣殿

最著名的故事，是所罗门驱使恶魔搬运巨石、开凿山岩，以修建耶路撒冷圣殿。传说恶魔之首亚什莫代（Asmodeus，即图鉴中的第 32 柱）也曾被所罗门俘获，为其服役。这些故事把「神圣的建筑工程」与「被征服的黑暗力量」编织在一起，成为后世魔典的核心意象。

The best-known tale has Solomon pressing demons into hauling stone and hewing rock to raise the Temple in Jerusalem. Even Asmodeus — the 32nd spirit of the catalogue — was said to have been captured and made to serve. These stories weave together sacred construction and conquered darkness, the core image of the later grimoires.

铜瓶

圣殿落成后，传说所罗门将不顺服的恶魔尽数封入一口铜瓶，以神名封印，沉入巴比伦的深渊。这一意象后来在《一千零一夜》中化为「渔夫与魔鬼」的著名故事——一个被封印千年的精灵从瓶中释放。它承载着一层核心观念：恶魔可以被「召唤、约束与封印」，这正是后世魔法手册的前提。

When the Temple was done, Solomon is said to have sealed the disobedient spirits in a brazen vessel, closed with the Name, and cast it into the deeps of Babylon. The image passed into the Arabian Nights as "The Fisherman and the Jinni" — a spirit freed after a thousand years. It carries the central premise of the later handbooks: that demons may be summoned, bound and sealed.

名字为何归于所罗门

严格说，《所罗门之钥》与《所罗门的小钥匙》都不是所罗门本人所写——它们成书于十四至十七世纪，属于「伪经」（pseudepigrapha），即假托古代圣贤之名以增权威。中世纪与文艺复兴的抄写者借用所罗门之名，把散落的召魔技艺编纂成体系，使所罗门成为「魔法师之王」的文化符号。

Strictly, neither Key was written by Solomon — they were compiled between the fourteenth and seventeenth centuries as pseudepigrapha, works borrowing an ancient sage's name for authority. Medieval and Renaissance scribes gathered scattered conjuring arts under Solomon's name, fixing him as the cultural figure of the magician-king.
