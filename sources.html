口径说明

七十二天使（Shem HaMephorash）之名与度数为卡巴拉传统中的通行记载，但各来源在拼写与属性上略有差异。本站的「恶魔↔天使」逐位对应（第 1 柱对第 1 天使，以此类推）属于现代阐释性对应，并非古代正典。封印图因年代版本不同，个别形态可能有细微出入。

The names and degrees of the seventy-two angels (Shem HaMephorash) follow common Kabbalistic usage, though sources vary in spelling and attributes. The one-to-one pairing of demon and angel here (1st spirit with 1st angel, and so on) is a modern interpretive correspondence, not ancient canon. Individual seal forms may vary slightly across manuscript versions.

学习声明

本站内容仅为历史、文化与文学之学习用途，不宣扬、亦不指导任何仪式实践。所罗门魔典是文艺复兴与近代早期欧洲神秘学传统的产物，其价值在于理解一种思想的谱系。

This site is offered for historical, cultural and literary study alone; it endorses and instructs no ritual practice. The Solomonic grimoires are products of the Renaissance and early-modern European occult tradition, and their worth lies in understanding a genealogy of ideas.
