The Lesser Key

小钥匙 · 五部书

Lemegeton Clavicula Salomonis —— 十七世纪汇编，将「所罗门掌控恶魔」的传说系统化为五部。

概述

《所罗门的小钥匙》（Lemegeton，又名 Clavicula Salomonis Regis）成书于十七世纪，是一部五合一的汇编。它把此前散落各处的召灵文本——尤其是约翰·维耶（Johann Weyer）1563 年《伪单子恶魔论》中的恶魔名录——整合成一套完整的仪式体系。全书五部性质迥异，从召魔到祈神，几乎囊括了近代早期欧洲「灵体魔法」的全部面向。

The Lesser Key of Solomon (Lemegeton, or Clavicula Salomonis Regis) was compiled in the seventeenth century as a five-part anthology. It gathers earlier texts on spirit conjuration — above all the catalogue of demons in Johann Weyer's Pseudomonarchia Daemonum (1563) — into one complete system. Its five books differ widely in character, from demon-summoning to prayer, covering nearly the whole range of early-modern spirit magic.

Ars Goetia · 召唤术

五部中最著名的一部，列有 72 位恶魔的名讳、位阶、封印与召唤法，是后世恶魔学的母本。每一位恶魔都有其爵位（王、公爵、亲王、侯爵、伯爵、骑士或统领）、所率军团数目、以及特定的权能与召唤时辰。本站在「七十二柱」页为它们逐一立传。

The most famous of the five, listing the seventy-two demons with their names, ranks, seals and rites of summoning — the mother of all later demonology. Each spirit has its rank (king, duke, prince, marquis, earl, knight or president), its number of legions, and its particular powers and hours. This site gives each of them an entry under "七十二柱".

Ars Theurgia-Goetia · 神术召唤

记述 31 位「空气之灵」及其众多下属。这些灵体兼具天使与精灵的双重性质，分属东、西、南、北四方，各有其主。与 Goetia 的恶魔相比，它们更接近可被召唤的「半神」或「元素王」，基调也更偏中性乃至良善。

Describes thirty-one "aerial spirits" and their many subordinates. These beings mingle angelic and elemental natures, divided among the four quarters of the compass, each under its own prince. Compared with the demons of the Goetia, they read more like summonable "demigods" or elemental kings, and their tone is more neutral or even benign.

Ars Paulina · 保罗术

与黄道星座和行星时辰相关的天使召唤。传说此术源自使徒保罗被提至第三重天时所得的启示。它把 360 度黄道分为十二宫，每一宫、每一度都有对应的天使，供施术者在特定时日召唤。

Angelic conjurations tied to the signs of the zodiac and the planetary hours. Legend traces the art to the Apostle Paul's rapture to the third heaven. It divides the 360 degrees of the ecliptic among the twelve signs, assigning angels to each sign and degree to be invoked on their appointed days.

Ars Almadel · 阿尔马德尔

记载四方四天使的召唤，借由一方名为「阿尔马德尔」的方形蜡板进行。蜡板以特定材料与工序制成，施术者透过它沟通四位天使，求得所愿。

Records the invocation of the four angels of the four quarters by means of a square waxen tablet called the "Almadel." Made of prescribed materials and rites, the tablet serves as the medium through which the four angels are addressed.

Ars Notoria · 诺托里亚

五部中最古老、也最独特的一部。它不含召魔，而是一套祈祷与「注视图」的体系，相传出自所罗门本人，用于求得知识、口才与记忆——中世纪学者称之为「所罗门的诺托里亚术」。它本质上是祈求智慧的宗教实践，与其余四部的召灵截然不同。

The oldest and most singular of the five. It contains no demon-summoning, but a system of prayers and "notae" — pictorial figures — attributed to Solomon himself, for obtaining knowledge, eloquence and memory. Medieval scholars called it the "Notory Art of Solomon." It is essentially a devotional practice for wisdom, quite unlike the conjurations of the other four books.
