小钥匙 · 五部书

Lemegeton Clavicula Salomonis —— 十七世纪汇编，将「所罗门掌控恶魔」的传说系统化为五部。

概述

《所罗门的小钥匙》（Lemegeton，又名 Clavicula Salomonis Regis）成书于十七世纪，是一部五合一的汇编。它把此前散落各处的召灵文本——尤其是约翰·维耶（Johann Weyer）1563 年《伪单子恶魔论》中的恶魔名录——整合成一套完整的仪式体系。全书五部性质迥异，从召魔到祈神，几乎囊括了近代早期欧洲「灵体魔法」的全部面向。

The Lesser Key of Solomon (Lemegeton, or Clavicula Salomonis Regis) was compiled in the seventeenth century as a five-part anthology. It gathers earlier texts on spirit conjuration — above all the catalogue of demons in Johann Weyer's Pseudomonarchia Daemonum (1563) — into one complete system. Its five books differ widely in character, from demon-summoning to prayer, covering nearly the whole range of early-modern spirit magic.

Ars Goetia · 召唤术

五部中最著名的一部，列有 72 位恶魔的名讳、位阶、封印与召唤法，是后世恶魔学的母本。每一位恶魔都有其爵位（王、公爵、亲王、侯爵、伯爵、骑士或统领）、所率军团数目、以及特定的权能与召唤时辰。本站在「七十二柱」页为它们逐一立传。

The most famous of the five, listing the seventy-two demons with their names, ranks, seals and rites of summoning — the mother of all later demonology. Each spirit has its rank (king, duke, prince, marquis, earl, knight or president), its number of legions, and its particular powers and hours. This site gives each of them an entry under "七十二柱".

Ars Theurgia-Goetia · 神术召唤

记述 31 位「空气之灵」及其众多下属。这些灵体兼具天使与精灵的双重性质，分属东、西、南、北四方，各有其主。与 Goetia 的恶魔相比，它们更接近可被召唤的「半神」或「元素王」，基调也更偏中性乃至良善。

Describes thirty-one "aerial spirits" and their many subordinates. These beings mingle angelic and elemental natures, divided among the four quarters of the compass, each under its own prince. Compared with the demons of the Goetia, they read more like summonable "demigods" or elemental kings, and their tone is more neutral or even benign.

Ars Paulina · 保罗术

与黄道星座和行星时辰相关的天使召唤。传说此术源自使徒保罗被提至第三重天时所得的启示。它把 360 度黄道分为十二宫，每一宫、每一度都有对应的天使，供施术者在特定时日召唤。

Angelic conjurations tied to the signs of the zodiac and the planetary hours. Legend traces the art to the Apostle Paul's rapture to the third heaven. It divides the 360 degrees of the ecliptic among the twelve signs, assigning angels to each sign and degree to be invoked on their appointed days.

Ars Almadel · 阿尔马德尔

记载四方四天使的召唤，借由一方名为「阿尔马德尔」的方形蜡板进行。蜡板以特定材料与工序制成，施术者透过它沟通四位天使，求得所愿。

Records the invocation of the four angels of the four quarters by means of a square waxen tablet called the "Almadel." Made of prescribed materials and rites, the tablet serves as the medium through which the four angels are addressed.

Ars Notoria · 诺托里亚

五部中最古老、也最独特的一部。它不含召魔，而是一套祈祷与「注视图」的体系，相传出自所罗门本人，用于求得知识、口才与记忆——中世纪学者称之为「所罗门的诺托里亚术」。它本质上是祈求智慧的宗教实践，与其余四部的召灵截然不同。

The oldest and most singular of the five. It contains no demon-summoning, but a system of prayers and "notae" — pictorial figures — attributed to Solomon itself, for obtaining knowledge, eloquence and memory. Medieval scholars called it the "Notory Art of Solomon." It is essentially a devotional practice for wisdom, quite unlike the conjurations of the other four books.

Goetia 的操作：三段召词与逐级加码

Ars Goetia 的召唤程序是五部书中最精密的。施术者立于圆内，面向灵应现的方位，手持佩剑或指环，依次可用三段召词，一段比一段强硬：第一段以「至高神、永恒神」与四王之名，以恳请的口吻召他前来；第二段奉创造他的那位神与「以赛亚所见之三重圣哉」的名，严令他必须在三角中显现；第三段则是最后通牒——以 Ehyeh、Tetragrammaton、Adonai 等最可畏的名起誓，若再不来，便将他押往那「直到末日才受审」的深渊，永不复见天光。三段之间，若灵迟迟不至，文本还备有「变名之法」：调换神名的排列重念；以及诅咒与约束四王的辅助召词。出现之后，程序转入核验：要灵出示忠诚的保证、承诺不伤害施术者与旁观者、如实回答，最后以固定的遣散词放归。整套文本的语气与其说是狂喜，不如说是庭审。

The working of the Ars Goetia is the most elaborate of the five. The operator stands within the circle, faces the quarter from which the spirit must appear, ring or sword in hand, and may escalate through three forms of conjuration. The first, in the name of the Highest and Eternal God and of the four kings, asks him to come. The second, by the God who made him and by the thrice-holy Name seen by Isaiah, strictly commands him to show himself within the triangle. The third is an ultimatum: sworn by the most terrible Names — Ehyeh, Tetragrammaton, Adonai — that if he delays longer he shall be delivered to the bottomless pit, kept until the day of judgment. Between the forms, if he tarries, the text supplies the "mutation of the Names" — reordering and repeating them — plus curses and an adjuration of the four kings. Once he appears, the procedure moves to verification: bonds of loyalty, a promise of no harm, true answers; at the end, the fixed licence to depart releases him. The register throughout is not ecstasy but court hearing.

四王与方位的行政

Goetia 的空间由四位「大王」分管：东方 Amaymon、西方 Paymon、南方 Zepar、北方 Barson。每位王统辖其方位下的诸王、公爵与侯爵——召唤词里「我命令你，并命东方之王 Amaymon、西方之王 Paymon……」一句，正是借四王之权对下属施压；若某灵不服，还可单独召其方位之王「传唤」他。这套设计的深意在于：恶魔并非一盘散沙的混沌，而是一个有辖区、有层级、可越级申诉的官僚体系。Theurgia-Goetia 部里，四王更各配「四位小王」与数十位空气之灵，把这张管辖图扩展到了三百余员。

Goetic space is administered by four great kings: Amaymon of the East, Paymon of the West, Zepar of the South, Barson of the North. Each governs the kings, dukes and marquises of his quarter — and when the adjuration runs "I command thee, and I command the King of the East, Amaymon; the King of the West, Paymon…," it is precisely the kings' delegated authority being brought to bear on a recalcitrant subordinate; a refractory spirit may be summoned through his own quarter-king. The design's implication is that the demons are no chaotic swarm but a bureaucracy of districts, ranks and appeal — a map the Ars Theurgia-Goetia extends past three hundred officers by giving each great king four lesser kings and whole troops of aerial spirits.

抄本与流传：一部「汇编的汇编」

《小钥匙》没有作者，只有编纂者。它的五部来自不同世纪：Notoria 最古（十三、四世纪），Goetia 的主体承自维耶 1563 年的名录，Theurgia-Goetia、Paulina、Almadel 多为十六、十七世纪的产物——把它们缝合成「所罗门的小钥匙」的，是一位十七世纪的匿名汇编者（有学者推测是某位都柏林的抄写人）。现存最早的完整抄本藏于大英图书馆（Sloane 3824，十七世纪中叶）；1889 年 Mathers 将其译出，1904 年 Crowley 补注出版并附上长篇「释论」（Scholia），用佛教与瑜伽的概念逐条评注每一位恶魔——这版「Mathers–Crowley」成了此后一切流行版本的底本，本站封印图所据的 Peterson 校勘本亦在其中。换句话说，今天人们谈论的「七十二柱」，其实是一部假托所罗门、经由维耶、汇编于十七世纪、定型于二十世纪初的层累文本。

The Lesser Key has no author, only compilers. Its five books span centuries: the Notoria oldest (thirteenth or fourteenth), the Goetia's roster descended from Weyer's 1563 list, the Theurgia-Goetia, Paulina and Almadel mostly sixteenth- and seventeenth-century work — stitched into "the Lesser Key of Solomon" by an anonymous compiler of the mid-seventeenth century (one scholar suggested a Dublin scrivener). The earliest complete manuscript survives in the British Library (Sloane 3824); S.L. MacGregor Mathers translated it in 1889, and Aleister Crowley's 1904 edition added a long commentary, the Scholia, annotating each spirit with concepts drawn from Buddhism and yoga. That Mathers–Crowley text became the stock of every popular version since, including the edition of Joseph H. Peterson from which our seals come. The "seventy-two pillars" people discuss today are thus a layered text: pseudo-Solomonic, mediated by Weyer, compiled in the 1600s, fixed in the early 1900s.
